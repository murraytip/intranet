# Manual Histology at MCCF
Our staff can train and guide you through all the steps of tissue processing and detection of molecules of interest in their natural environment.

We can aid with:

- [Tissue processing](tissueprocessing.html)
- [Histological staining](histologicalstaining.html)
- [Manual *in situ* detection](manualdetection.html)

We can also advise and assist on all steps of tissue clearing and expansion microscopy using a variety of different methods including CUBIC and iDISCO-based protocols. We also offer spatial transcriptomics assays on tissue sections in collaboration with IGO and SAIL, using the Visium and Xenium Spatial Transcriptomics platforms from [10x Genomics](https://www.10xgenomics.com).
